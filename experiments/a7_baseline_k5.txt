 A7 fault injection: provider under-reporting vs cap-respecting
==========================================================================
Cap B_0 = 2000 uc | margin 2.0x | 1000 trials/k

    k    overshoot               95% CI   mean over%   max over%
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  5.0   211/1000   [0.187, 0.237]          53.7%      241.2%

Interpretation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
At k=5.0 (provider under-reports 5x): 211/1000 trials overshoot, mean 53.7% over cap.
The discipline cannot detect this: it only sees reported
charges, so the ledger admits calls the true cost has already
exhausted. This is the A7 trust-boundary limitation conceded
in the limitations section, now quantified.

Paragraph for the paper (section 8.6 / threats):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  To quantify the A7 (charge-truthfulness) trust boundary, we ran a
  fault-injection experiment (1000 trials per condition,
  cap 2000 uc) simulating a provider that under-reports
  usage by a factor k. At k=1 the discipline is cap-respecting
  (0 overshoot). At k=[worst k], it overshoots on [N]/[trials]
  sessions by a mean of [X]%, undetectably, because the ledger
  observes only reported charges. This confirms A7 as a genuine
  limitation shared with all client-side accounting; [if you ran
  --reconcile-every:] periodic ground-truth reconciliation bounds
  the overshoot to the inter-reconciliation window.
